# Post-race news

Mode: Read. Local extension of the established game interface; user specified headline, byline, finish photo, article, and Results > Next > News. Code-led workflow retained.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Turn the just-completed race into a readable report, led by its real finish photograph rather than another statistics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit charcoal surfaces, red actions, Barlow and Barlow Condensed, existing corners and focus treatment. No new visual identity or token changes.

STORY: Review results, choose Next, read the driver's series-specific report, then return to Career or the main menu. Four user-named reporters rotate. Career championship facts are current after classification; exhibition reports make no championship claims.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered reading column with Race News header, large result-specific headline, byline, full-width 16:9 finish photograph and plain caption. Article text follows at a comfortable measure; a full-width continuation action ends the report. Mobile keeps the same order with fluid type.

FORM: Precisely specified extension, no concept seed needed. The signature is a single trackside photograph taken at the leader's crossing, independent of chase cameras and HUD. Simulation/capture failure is an explicit unavailable state, never a staged finish labeled live.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
