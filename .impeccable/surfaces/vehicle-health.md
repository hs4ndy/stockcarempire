# Crashes and vehicle health

Mode: Operate. Precisely specified local extension of the existing HUD, Quick Race chooser, Garage and Settings. Existing world and code-led preference retained; no concept seed for this extension.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Separate immediate race health, repairable garage condition and permanent car life clearly, without crowding the race or hiding the switches.

OWN-WORLD: Existing charcoal/graphite, Barlow typography, red actions, green health and restrained shared controls. No new brand, decorative imagery or panels outside existing component grammar.

STORY: Choose eligible rules, race with readable health beside speed/draft, recover after an incident, repair condition, track lifetime health in Garage and replace retired cars while keeping their records.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Health sits inside the existing single-player telemetry; both split panes show their own compact percentage. Settings and Quick Race use ordinary checkbox rows with explanatory copy and disabled easy-mode state. Garage displays lifetime health and a visible graveyard button. Graveyard is a bounded scrolling dialog with retained stats.

FORM: Local code-led extension. User confirmed default on for Semi-Pro/Pro/All-Out, one damage switch for race/lifetime wear, about40 starts in every series, zero health slows without spinning when crashes are off. Safe bumper drafting must not accumulate crash strain.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
