These are **fictional brands and initial tuning values**, not verified economic balance.

All amounts below are **per completed race, before Commercial Director adjustments**.

Performance bonuses:

- **Primary:** an additional **50% of base pay for a win**.
- **Secondary:** an additional **25% for a top-five finish**.
- **Tertiary:** an additional **25% for a top-10 finish**.
- Bonuses use the actual classified player finish. They do not stack within one contract.
- Round final contract amounts to whole dollars.

The winning-debut primary offers are **Copperline Tools**, **Redline Fabrication**, and **Vanguard Motors**, respectively.
